Repeated transaction numbers

A repeated transaction number is one Transaction No used on more than one payment line inside a single quarterly file. Lines are counted within that file. The same number appearing again in a later quarter is counted separately, below.

Most repeats are several lines for one supplier: the same purpose more than once, or different purposes. A number shared by different suppliers is a separate case.

Original files

All 22 original files contain at least one repeated number: 632 numbers, out of 16112. Of those, 401 are the same supplier and the same purpose, and 229 are the same supplier with more than one purpose. Different suppliers share a number twice, both in the 2025/26 Q2 file, Second-Quarter-July-2025-September-2025-CSVin.csv: 400008674 (5 lines, 3 suppliers, 1 August 2025) and 400008885 (10 lines, 9 suppliers, 1 October 2025).

Two numbers also appear in two original quarters, with the same supplier and amount both times: 300016904, AECOM, £6,100, in 2024/25 Q3 and Q4, and 300016921, Turner and Townsend, £1,062, in the same two quarters. Every other original transaction number stays inside one quarter.
